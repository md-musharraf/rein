What Rein does

For students, developers and anyone who opens one app and loses an hour.

Blocks the feed, not the app

Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, TikTok and X video feeds are recognised on screen and blocked. Messages, search and long videos keep working.

Scroll and time limits

Allow yourself a few reels a day, or a number of minutes per app. A shorter limit applies at once; a longer one waits until tomorrow, so you can never buy more time in the moment.

Focus timer

Pomodoro plans (Classic, Deep Work, Sprint), study subjects and a strict mode for exams and deep work, with a desk clock you can read on the lock screen.

Schedules

Study hours, work hours, bedtime. Distracting feeds shut automatically when the window starts.

Adult content filter

Optional and on-device. It works as a local DNS filter, so no traffic is sent to any server, and you can switch it off.

Screen time stats

Where your day went, per app, with a weekly chart and CSV export. The data stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall.

Frequently asked questions

How do I block Instagram Reels without deleting Instagram?

Install Rein and turn on its Accessibility Service. Reels is blocked; DMs, stories, search and profiles keep working.

Can I block YouTube Shorts but still watch normal videos?

Yes. Only the Shorts player is blocked. Long videos, playlists and subscriptions work as usual.

Is the Accessibility permission safe? Does Rein read my messages?

Rein only checks whether the screen is a short-video feed. Nothing on your screen is saved or sent anywhere, and Rein has no servers.

Will Rein block UPI or banking apps?

No. When a payment or banking app opens, Rein removes any block, so PIN screens are never covered.

Why did blocking stop working?

Some phones stop background apps to save battery. Rein warns you when protection has stopped and shows how to switch it back on and remove the battery restriction.

Can I turn Rein off?

Yes, any time. It takes 15 minutes, so the decision is yours and not the urge's.

Is Rein free?

Yes. Everything is free during beta, and anyone who installs during beta keeps full access for life.

Which phones does Rein work on?

Android 7.0 and newer, including Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Vivo, Oppo, Realme and Pixel.
